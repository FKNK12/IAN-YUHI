WARRANTY POLICY

Effective Date: 10/01/2026

IAN YUHI stands behind the quality of our tailoring and craftsmanship. We want every customer to feel confident in the products they purchase from us.

1. Coverage

Our warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship for the product purchased from IAN YUHI. This includes defects that arise under normal use and proper care.

2. Exclusions

This warranty does not cover damage caused by normal wear and tear, improper care, accidents, misuse, alterations by unauthorized third parties, or damage caused after delivery.

3. Warranty Period

Warranty coverage begins on the date of delivery and lasts for a period determined by the product category. Please refer to your product details for specific coverage information.

4. Claim Process

If you believe your item has a defect, please contact us with your order number, a description of the issue, and photographs if possible. We will review the claim and advise on the next steps.

5. Resolution

Depending on the situation, we may offer repair, replacement, or store credit in accordance with the warranty terms and applicable law.
