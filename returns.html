RETURNS POLICY

Effective Date: 10/01/2026

We want you to be fully satisfied with your IAN YUHI purchase. If for any reason you are not happy with your order, we offer a straightforward returns process.

1. Return Window

Items may be returned within a specified period after delivery, provided they are unused, unworn, and in their original condition.

2. Eligibility

To be eligible for a return, the item must be in the same condition you received it, with original tags and packaging where applicable.

3. Non-Returnable Items

Custom-made, personalized, or altered items may not be eligible for return unless there is a defect or issue with the product itself.

4. Return Process

To initiate a return, please contact our customer support team with your order details. We will provide instructions and a return authorization if approved.

5. Refunds

Once the return is approved and the item is received, refunds will be processed in accordance with the original payment method and our internal review process.
