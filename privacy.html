PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: 10/01/2026

Last Updated: 10/01/2030

Welcome to IAN YUHI (“we,” “our,” or “us”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us when you visit our website, shop for our Anar kali suits and apparel, or interact with our services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies, relates to, describes, or is reasonably capable of being associated with you (“Personal Information”).

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and billing address.
  • Account Information: Username, password, order history, and saved preferences or wishlists.
  • Payment Information: Payment card details, billing address, and transaction details. (Note: We do not store full credit card numbers directly; payment transactions are processed through secure third-party payment processors.)
  • Customer Support & Communications: Information provided when contacting customer service, submitting reviews, or responding to surveys.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, time spent on pages, and click behavior.
  • Cookies & Tracking Technologies: Cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to enhance navigation, analyze site traffic, and personalize content.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following business purposes:

  • Order Processing & Fulfillment: Processing transactions, shipping orders, sending order confirmations, and providing delivery updates.
  • Customer Service: Responding to inquiries, handling returns/exchanges, and providing support.
  • Personalization: Tailoring product recommendations, promotions, and content based on shopping preferences.
  • Marketing Communications: Sending promotional emails, newsletters, and special offers (you may opt out at any time).
  • Analytics & Performance: Improving site functionality, user experience, and optimizing store operations.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: Detecting, preventing, and prosecuting fraudulent transactions and unauthorized activities.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data with trusted third parties under the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who perform services on our behalf (e.g., payment gateways, shipping and courier companies, email delivery services, website analytics).
  • Legal Compliance & Protection: When required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, and safety of our business, customers, or others.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, asset sale, or reorganization.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to:

  • Remember items added to your shopping cart.
  • Store your login session and site preferences.
  • Analyze traffic patterns and user interactions.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may impact certain site features or shopping functionalities.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical, administrative, and physical security measures designed to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system is 100% secure.

6. Your Data Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access & Request: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain legal obligations.
  • Opt-Out of Marketing: Unsubscribe from marketing emails by clicking the “unsubscribe” link in any email or by contacting us directly.

7. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party sites or social media platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

Our products and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16, depending on jurisdiction). If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
